## Generalisation, alternatives and selection

A full quality management method must justify each mechanism by its contribution to outcomes; making an unsupported mechanism optional does not supply justification. Removing an unsupported technique requires separating necessary system responsibilities from replaceable syntax. Ordinary-English requirements still need enough meaning to be interpreted and checked. Compare (a) retain a core pillar: unsupported; (b) optional retained reference: still clutter without evidence; (c) lexical deletion only: could leave an ambiguity/verification hole; (d) semantic removal plus appropriate existing requirements/analysis/authority controls. Select(d), under explicit owner mandate.

PTE sources identify seven substantive responsibilities: actors, technical terms, relevant conditions, timing, quantities, failure responses, observable acceptance.
