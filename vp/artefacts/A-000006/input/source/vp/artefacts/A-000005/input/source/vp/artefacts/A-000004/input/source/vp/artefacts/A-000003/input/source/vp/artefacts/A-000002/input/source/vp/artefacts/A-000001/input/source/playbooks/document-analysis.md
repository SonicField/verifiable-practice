# Document Analysis

Analyse a document, proposal, idea, or communication by separating what it says from what it rewards or causes.

## Process

1. Acquire the complete target and establish its provenance, audience, date, and context.
2. Read [Goals](../concepts/goals.md), [Rhetoric](../concepts/rhetoric.md), [Bullshit Detection](../concepts/bullshit-detection.md), and [Falsifiability](../concepts/falsifiability.md).
3. Extract stated goals using direct quotations.
4. Infer actual goals from incentives, resource allocation, requested actions, omissions, and who benefits.
5. Separate Ethos, Pathos, and Logos. Identify where one is presented as another.
6. List consequential claims and the evidence that would falsify each one.
7. Compare stated and actual goals. Explain the practical consequence of any gap.
8. Invite correction where intent cannot be established from the document.

## Output

```markdown
# Analysis: [Title]

## Context and Provenance
## Stated Goals
## Actual Incentives and Effects
## Gap Between Them
## Rhetoric: Ethos, Pathos, Logos
## Unfalsifiable or Unsupported Claims
## Who Benefits and Who Bears the Cost
## Unknowns and Alternative Interpretations
## Verdict
```

## Rules

- Quote before interpreting.
- Do not mistake disagreement for deception.
- Do not claim hidden intent when incentives or effects are all the evidence supports.
- Include the strongest reasonable alternative interpretation.
- Distinguish falsehood, uncertainty, and indifference to truth.
