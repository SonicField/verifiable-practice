## Actual incentives and effects

README tells a newcomer to read eight concepts before its project-record section. WHY spends most of its explanation on adversarial testing and philosophical concepts. The overview offers a short task loop and a three-layer packaging model. Those choices plausibly direct readers toward reasoning improvement and a reusable AI skill; the full management lifecycle requires following later references and assembling the model themselves.

These are observable presentation choices and likely effects, not proof of hidden motives or measured reader behaviour. The owner has now explicitly supplied the intended broader identity.

## Gap between them

The operational method already exists, but primary entry documents do not explain it as a connected whole. Management is subordinate to epistemology or packaging. A newcomer could reasonably understand “use VP” as performing better tests and honest summaries without establishing a charter, management roles, controlled stages, persistent records, revalidation, custody or independent acceptance.

## Findings and observable closure conditions

### CQ-001 — Human and AI entry points understate the system

**Severity:** high for the owner's communication goal. README and SKILL lead with reasoning/testing; “Start Here” first sends readers through eight concepts. README later says “For projects that need an auditable history” before introducing records. The strongest alternative reading is that modular progressive disclosure deliberately keeps the entry concise and a determined reader can find all controls. That is reasonable, but does not make the whole method immediately identifiable.

**Closure:** README and SKILL discovery/opening explicitly identify the full project quality management system and explain its governing lifecycle/roles. Concrete startup directs project users to charter/plan/state/ledger/coverage, with foundations available as supporting learning. Preserve task routing and explicit adoption; do not force every reference into every task context.

### CQ-002 — WHY's summary denies the prescribed management contract

**Severity:** high. WHY's “does not prescribe one way to work” is a reasonable statement about proportional engineering techniques, but the standard prescribes mandatory documents, before-action records and independent gates for adopted projects. WHY presents one function/testing loop and does not explain what manages a continuing project.

**Closure:** WHY explains why collaboration needs authority, planning, choices, retained evidence, acceptance, change/expiry and handoff controls. Its summary distinguishes flexible technical methods from explicit adopted management/record requirements. The worked testing example remains useful within that lifecycle rather than defining the whole system.

### CQ-003 — Overview and architecture substitute distribution layers for management

**Severity:** high. [How VP Works](../C-000004/before/source/docs/how-vp-works.md) describes “The Three Layers” as concepts, playbooks and root skill. [Skill Architecture](../C-000004/before/source/docs/skill-architecture.md) describes distribution well, but omits the instantiated vp root from its tree. ORIGIN's extraction exclusions include supervisor/worker/etc. team roles; it later explains independent record extension, but the current distinction between management responsibilities and excluded runtime machinery is insufficiently prominent.

**Closure:** overview gives the whole management lifecycle and responsibilities with authoritative routes, including planning/assurance/control/maintenance. Architecture identifies concepts, procedures, standards/templates, records/evidence and skill as distinct support for the method. ORIGIN preserves extraction history while explicitly distinguishing current portable owner/worker/auditor roles from excluded persistent team software.

### CQ-004 — Collaboration roles and authority need an integrated account

**Severity:** medium/high. The zero-code contract supplies a default human-owner/AI-machinist pattern and links AI-owner mandates, but primary introductions omit who tracks VP and who independently assesses it. Goals says understanding what people want “not what they said, not what they wrote in the specification” is foundational, which can encourage unrecorded inferred intent overriding the explicit mandate. A charitable interpretation is that it asks for deeper clarification, not permission expansion; the text should state that boundary.

**Closure:** owner controls goal/mandate/scope/criteria/tolerances; worker performs authorised work and records; distinct auditor assesses record discipline without implementation. Explain AI owners require bounded originating authority and tracking remains worker responsibility under owner oversight. Present zero-code as one collaboration arrangement, not a prohibition on other agreed human/AI contributions. Explicit instructions remain authority; uncertain intent prompts clarification instead of silent replacement.

### CQ-005 — Empirical falsifiers are conflated with management authority and value

**Severity:** medium. Falsifiability says “Any claim worth making” needs a counterexample and criticises “NO EXCEPTIONS” as beyond falsification. The record standard §4 correctly treats goals/value judgements differently from empirical technical hypotheses. Management also needs binding authority/acceptance boundaries; an experiment does not grant permission to violate them.

**Closure:** distinguish empirical claims (test with falsifiable protocols) from goals/values/mandates/constraints (record authority, rationale and authorised review). Rules may be reviewable without being promises of empirical truth. Preserve mandatory role/permission/audit boundaries and the user's explicit instructions.

### CQ-006 — Related guides describe extensions rather than a coherent full method

**Severity:** medium. Project Records opens “VP's reasoning loop needs a durable implementation”, and documentation is repeatedly labelled human-facing while playbooks alone direct AI execution. Specialised guides are valuable and need not repeat every lifecycle step, but their position in the complete system is unclear. CONTRIBUTING's opening also repeats the smaller reasoning-first identity.

**Closure:** related guide introductions identify which part of the full method they implement and route back to a coherent overview. Humans and AI can both learn from explanations; procedures are still separate from rationale. Preserve narrow modules, tool neutrality, existing example provenance and adopted standard's actual scope.

## Rhetoric: ethos, pathos and logos

The source uses provocative “bullshit” language and philosophy to motivate evidence discipline (pathos). The worked example and records standard provide actionable reasoning (logos). Invoking PRINCE2/CMM/aircraft tracking supplies design inspiration, not certification or demonstrated superior effectiveness (ethos with limits). Stronger full-system language needs an actual lifecycle/role/action map so a label does not become unsupported authority.

## Unfalsifiable or unsupported claims

This assessment cannot establish that every human or AI will understand the method, that a full management system produces better outcomes, or that the original authors intended the current positioning. Existing scientific/software examples, legal metadata and broad AI behaviour statements are outside this communication audit's technical scope. The repair must state actual process coverage while retaining research/efficacy and conformance/product-quality distinctions.

## Who benefits and who bears the cost

Owners, workers and future auditors benefit from clear starting actions and visible responsibility. Maintainers bear additional navigation/explanation upkeep. Long repetitive introductions or loading every document would burden readers and AI context; keep one coherent overview and purpose-specific routes.

## Unknowns and alternative interpretations

A careful full reader can already recover most controls from the standard and guides. This is undercommunication and some local contradictions, not evidence that the project lacks management machinery. No reader survey was performed. A complete document-quality audit of every scientific/software assertion would be a different, wider commission.

## Verdict

**The full management method is substantially implemented, but the public explanation foregrounds epistemology and task-level practice. It does not yet satisfy the owner's Q1–Q10 communication goal as a connected whole.** Preserve this baseline report and sources. The owner already authorised repairs; implement them under a new design decision and verify actual current text and routes, with explicit limits. No independent Project Audit or product-correctness verdict is awarded here.
